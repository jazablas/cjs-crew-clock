Crew guide

CJ's Crew - Clock: clock in, take breaks, see your schedule.
App address: cjwtbk.com
Scan to open

1 Install the app

iPhone (Safari)
  • Scan the code or go to the address above.
  • Tap Share ↑, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android (Chrome)
  • Open the address, then tap Install when asked, or go to Menu ⋮ and tap Install app.
  • Open CJ's Crew from its icon on your home screen from now on.

2 Sign in

  • Your manager adds you with your mobile number and gives you a 6-digit PIN.
  • Enter your number, then your PIN. Turn on Keep me signed in on your own phone.
  • Never share your PIN. Five wrong tries locks sign-in for 15 minutes.
  • Forgot it? Ask a manager to reset it.

3 Clock in

  • Tap the sticker: unlock your phone and hold the top of it to the CJ's sticker at your venue.
  • Or open the app and tap Clock in. Allow location; it only works at the venue.
  • Take the quick selfie when asked. It proves it's you.
  • No phone? Use the wall tablet: tap Clock in, then enter your PIN.

4 Breaks

  • The app tells you when a 10-minute, 15-minute or meal break is due.
  • On the crew board, a name in red means that person's break is due. Check it, even without your phone.
  • Tap Start break and End break, or tap the sticker, so your time is right.
  • Missed a break because it was busy? Tell a manager the same day.

5 Clock out

  • Tap the sticker again or tap Clock out in the app, before you change or leave.
  • Check your hours anytime in Timesheet.
Made a mistake?
  • Forgot to punch? Use Missed punch on the Clock screen.
  • Wrong time? Open it in Timesheet and tap Request a correction. A manager approves it.

6 Schedule & more

  • Schedule: see your shifts, confirm them, offer a swap or pick up open shifts.
  • Availability & time off: set them in the app. Your manager approves requests.
  • Team: chat and announcements from managers.
  • Me: your pay rates, documents and the privacy notice.
