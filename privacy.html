CJ's Wings — The Broaster Kings ("CJ's", "we") uses CJ's Crew - Clock to run schedules, time clock, breaks, payroll prep and hiring. This notice explains what personal information the app collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how you can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

Why we use it

  • To schedule shifts, record hours and breaks accurately and pay you correctly, including overtime and break premiums.
  • To prevent buddy punching and time fraud (location, punch photos, device checks).
  • To meet California and federal employment, wage, safety and tax rules, and to answer audits or claims.
  • To review job applications and contact applicants.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to back it up.

We use your information only for these work purposes.

Who sees it

  • Managers and owners at CJ's see crew and applicant information. Other crew members see only what's needed to work together, like names on the schedule, who's on the clock and team chat. They never see your pay, phone number, photos, location or documents.
  • Service providers that run the app for us under contract: Supabase (secure database and file storage), HostGator (website hosting), GitHub (private, encrypted-in-transit backups), and Google Fonts (lettering).
  • Our payroll provider and accountant, for hours and pay.
  • Government agencies or courts when the law requires it.

We don't sell or rent personal information and don't share it for targeted advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Time, pay and schedule records (including punch photos and punch locations): at least 4 years after they're created, as California wage and tax rules require.
  • Employment documents such as I-9s: for as long as the law requires (for I-9s, 3 years after hire or 1 year after you leave, whichever is later).
  • Job applications from people we don't hire: 4 years, as California employment rules require, then deleted.
  • Team chat and requests: while you work with us and up to 1 year after.
  • Backups are kept for up to 6 months and then replaced automatically.

If you ask us to delete something we're legally required to keep, we'll tell you, limit its use to that legal purpose and delete it when the period ends.

How it's protected

  • All connections are encrypted (https).
  • PINs are hashed, sign-in locks after 5 wrong tries, and signing in on a new phone can be limited to approved devices.
  • Documents and resumes sit in private storage. They open only through short-lived links for managers or for the person they belong to.
  • Every manager action on sensitive data is recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we have about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your information, apart from records the law requires us to keep.
  • Stop using optional data, such as an emergency contact or a profile photo.

California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act where it applies. California law also lets current and former employees inspect or copy their personnel file and payroll records (Labor Code 226 and 1198.5). We won't retaliate against you for making a request. To confirm a request is really from you, we'll check it against the phone number you used with us. We may call or text you to verify it. We aim to respond within 10 business days, and no later than 45 days.
